GeoPace design mockups

Three directions for the same app, showing the same course, the same race plan and the same layers. Drag the kilometre strip (or focus it and use the arrow keys) and watch the readout, the clock and the shadows move together. Switch course, goal time and direction from the bar at the top of each page.

  1. Roadbook: a survey sheet. The course runs down the page as a route card; what runners say is written in the margin in purple pencil.
  2. Race poster: black, white and the blue line. Huge numerals on a strict grid; solid is measured, hollow is hearsay.
  3. Field instrument: a well log. Stacked tracks, one crosshair, the value under the cursor in every track's header.

Elevation, grade, difficulty and landmarks are real. Sun exposure, wind, aid stations and runner reports are invented placeholders, and are marked as samples wherever they appear. Unofficial — not affiliated with any race organizer.
